## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Types** | Polycythaemia vera (excess red cells), essential thrombocythaemia (platelets), myelofibrosis (marrow scarring) |
| **Mutations** | ***JAK2* V617F** (95% of polycythaemia), *CALR*, *MPL* |
| **Risks** | **Thrombosis**, bleeding, progression to myelofibrosis or leukaemia |
| **Treatment** | Aspirin, venesection, hydroxyurea, pegylated interferon, ruxolitinib and other JAK inhibitors |

## What they are

Marrow neoplasms in which a stem cell with a mutation activating the **JAK-STAT** pathway produces too many blood cells. They are chronic: many people live with them for decades. [Chronic myeloid leukaemia](../chronic-myeloid-leukaemia/) belongs to the same family but is treated separately.

| Disease | Features | Main treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Polycythaemia vera** | High haematocrit, itching after bathing, ruddy face, clots | Venesection (haematocrit < 45%), aspirin, hydroxyurea or **ropeginterferon** |
| **Essential thrombocythaemia** | High platelets, often no symptoms | Aspirin; hydroxyurea or anagrelide for high risk |
| **Myelofibrosis** | Anaemia, huge spleen, sweats, weight loss | **Ruxolitinib**, fedratinib, pacritinib, **momelotinib** (with anaemia); transplant |

## Advice

Control cardiovascular risk factors (smoking, blood pressure, cholesterol), stay active and hydrated, and seek help for symptoms of a clot (painful swollen leg, chest pain, neurological symptoms).

See also: [Chronic myeloid leukaemia](../chronic-myeloid-leukaemia/), [Myelodysplastic syndromes](../myelodysplastic-syndromes/).
