Today we launch **Oncolopedia**, the oncology encyclopedia of our network of sites, alongside Medicapedia, Farmacos.ai, Geneticapedia and Psycholopedia.

## Why

Almost everyone knows someone who has had cancer. In primary care the same questions come up again and again: what a report means, what immunotherapy is, whether to have a PSA test, whether sugar feeds tumours, how to explain it to the children. There is plenty of information online, but also a lot of noise, false promises and figures without context.

## What you will find

- **Profiles of the most common cancers** and those that raise the most questions.
- How **diagnosis** works and how to read a report.
- **Treatments**, from surgery to CAR-T and palliative care.
- **Prevention** and **screening**.
- **Living with cancer**: side effects, emotions, fertility, work and rights.
- An **atlas** of 273 malignant tumour subtypes, the **history** of oncology and its **people**, a **glossary** and a **quiz**.

All with **official and scientific sources**, and an update date.

## What you won't find

Oncolopedia does not offer diagnosis, treatment or individual second opinions, and does not replace your medical team. If you have questions about your case, ask them: what you read here can help you ask better questions.

If you find a mistake, please write to us.
