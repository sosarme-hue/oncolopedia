## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Children under 3; more in very low birth weight premature babies |
| **Syndromes** | Beckwith-Wiedemann, familial adenomatous polyposis (*APC*) |
| **Marker** | Very high **alpha-fetoprotein** |
| **Treatment** | Cisplatin and surgery; liver transplant if not resectable |
| **Survival** | Over 80% |

## Key points

It presents as a **swollen abdomen** or a mass. Alpha-fetoprotein is used for diagnosis and follow-up. International protocols (**PHITT**, PRETEXT staging) tailor treatment to risk: many children need only cisplatin and surgery; tumours that cannot be removed are treated with **liver transplant**, with excellent results. Hearing is monitored because of cisplatin ototoxicity (sodium thiosulfate reduces it).
