## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **How common** | 75% of renal cell carcinomas |
| **Key gene** | ***VHL*** inactivated in over 90% |
| **Inheritance** | Von Hippel-Lindau syndrome in a minority |
| **Treatment** | Partial or radical nephrectomy; adjuvant pembrolizumab; combined immunotherapy for advanced disease |

## Biology: pseudohypoxia

The ***VHL*** gene acts as a brake on **HIF** (hypoxia-inducible factors). When it is lost, the cell "thinks" it lacks oxygen and endlessly produces **VEGF** and other signals that create blood vessels. That is why the tumour is highly vascular and responds to **anti-angiogenic** drugs (sunitinib, pazopanib, axitinib, cabozantinib, lenvatinib, tivozanib) and **HIF-2α inhibitors** (belzutifan). The discovery of this pathway earned the **2019 Nobel Prize in Medicine** (Kaelin, Ratcliffe and Semenza).

## Von Hippel-Lindau syndrome

An inherited disease causing multiple, bilateral kidney tumours at young ages, haemangioblastomas of the nervous system and retina, phaeochromocytomas and pancreatic neuroendocrine tumours. **Belzutifan** shrinks these tumours and avoids repeated surgery.

## Outlook and risk groups

In advanced disease the **IMDC** classification is used, scoring time since diagnosis, general condition, anaemia, calcium, neutrophils and platelets: favourable, intermediate or poor risk.

## Treatment

- **Localised**: partial or radical nephrectomy; **pembrolizumab** for a year if relapse risk is high.
- **Advanced, first line**: nivolumab with ipilimumab, pembrolizumab with axitinib or lenvatinib, or nivolumab with cabozantinib. Some patients achieve lasting complete responses.
- **Later lines**: cabozantinib, **belzutifan** (LITESPARK-005 trial), lenvatinib with everolimus, tivozanib.
- **Cytoreductive nephrectomy** in selected cases; **stereotactic radiotherapy** or surgery for isolated metastases. Some metastases grow so slowly that they can be **watched** without treatment.
