## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **How common** | 20-25% of non-Hodgkin lymphomas; median age 60 |
| **Alteration** | t(14;18) translocation, *BCL2* overexpression |
| **Course** | Indolent, relapsing; 2-3% a year transform into aggressive lymphoma |
| **Treatment** | Monitoring; localised radiotherapy; rituximab with chemotherapy or lenalidomide; bispecifics and CAR-T |
| **Survival** | Median over 15-20 years |

## When to treat

In advanced stages without symptoms or bulky masses, **active monitoring** is as good as early treatment (GELF criteria). In localised stages I-II, **radiotherapy** can cure.

## Treatment

- **Rituximab or obinutuzumab with chemotherapy** (bendamustine or CHOP), with or without antibody maintenance.
- **Rituximab with lenalidomide** (R²).
- At relapse: **mosunetuzumab**, **epcoritamab**, glofitamab, tazemetostat, **CAR-T**.
- Relapse within 2 years (POD24): worse outlook, more intensive treatment.
