## A simple rule

A symptom that is **new**, **persistent** (more than two or three weeks) or **getting worse**, with no clear explanation, deserves a visit to the doctor. It will almost always have a benign cause, but seeing someone in time allows cancer to be ruled out or diagnosed earlier. Do not wait for your next screening round.

## Warning signs

| Symptom | Can be linked to |
|---|---|
| A new **lump** in the breast, testicle, neck, armpit or elsewhere | Breast, testicle, lymphomas, sarcomas and others |
| **Blood in your stools** or black stools, or a lasting change in bowel habit | Bowel, upper digestive tract |
| **Blood in your urine**, even once and without pain | Bladder, kidney |
| A **cough** lasting more than three weeks, or coughing blood; persistent hoarseness | Lung, larynx |
| **Difficulty swallowing** or persistent indigestion with weight loss | Oesophagus, stomach |
| **Vaginal bleeding** after menopause, between periods or after sex | Endometrium, cervix |
| A **mole that changes** or a sore that does not heal | Melanoma and other skin cancers |
| A **mouth ulcer** that has not healed in three weeks | Mouth cancer |
| Persistent **bloating** and feeling full quickly | Ovary |
| Unexplained **weight loss**, severe **tiredness**, **fever** or **night sweats** | Many cancers, lymphomas |
| **Jaundice** (yellow skin) | Pancreas, bile ducts, liver |
| A new, worsening **headache** with vomiting, or a seizure in an adult | Brain tumours |
| Unexplained bruising, bleeding or frequent infections | Leukaemias |

## In children

Pallor, bruising, prolonged fever, bone pain at night, limping, headaches with vomiting, a lump in the abdomen or a white reflection in the pupil in photos.

## When you see a doctor

Explain when it started, how it has changed and whether there is a family history. If the symptom continues or worsens despite a first treatment, go back. Many health systems have **fast-track pathways** for suspected cancer.
