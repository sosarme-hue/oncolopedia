Cancer types

Mouth cancer

Cancer of the lips, tongue, gums, floor of mouth and palate. A sore that does not heal in three weeks should be checked; dentists can spot it early.

At a glance
How common 390,000 cases a year worldwide; very common in South Asia
Causes Tobacco, alcohol, betel nut, sun (lip), poor oral hygiene
Precursor lesions Leukoplakia, erythroplakia, oral lichen planus, submucous fibrosis
Sign A sore, patch or lump in the mouth for more than 3 weeks
Treatment Surgery (with reconstruction) and radiotherapy or chemoradiotherapy

What it is

Oral cavity cancer includes the lip, the front two-thirds of the tongue, gums, floor of mouth, inner cheeks and hard palate. The tongue is the most common site. Unlike the oropharynx, HPV plays little role.

There is concern about rising tongue cancer in young people without tobacco or alcohol use, whose cause is being studied.

Precancerous lesions

  • Leukoplakia: a white patch that cannot be scraped off; 1-2% risk of becoming malignant per year, higher with dysplasia.
  • Erythroplakia: a red patch; high risk.
  • Oral lichen planus: an inflammatory disease with low risk.
  • Submucous fibrosis: from areca (betel) nut.

They are biopsied and monitored; stopping tobacco and alcohol lowers the risk.

Symptoms

A non-healing ulcer, white or red patch, lump, pain, bleeding, loose teeth without cause, dentures that stop fitting, difficulty moving the tongue or opening the mouth, a neck lump.

Diagnosis and treatment

  • Biopsy, CT or MRI, neck ultrasound.
  • Surgery with wide margins and neck dissection (or sentinel node for small tumours). Depth of invasion is key to staging.
  • Reconstruction with flaps (forearm, fibula for the jaw) by maxillofacial surgeons.
  • Radiotherapy or chemoradiotherapy after surgery depending on risk.
  • Rehabilitation of speech, chewing and swallowing; dental implants.

Outlook

Over 80% at five years in early stages; about 50% in advanced.

See also: Head and neck cancer, Oropharyngeal cancer, Cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma.
