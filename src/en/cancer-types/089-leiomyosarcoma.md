## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **How common** | 10-20% of soft tissue sarcomas |
| **Where** | Womb, retroperitoneum, inferior vena cava, limbs |
| **Treatment** | Surgery; doxorubicin (with trabectedin or dacarbazine), gemcitabine with docetaxel |

## Key points

It arises from smooth muscle fibres in the walls of vessels and hollow organs. **Uterine** leiomyosarcoma has [its own sheet](../uterine-sarcoma/). That of the **inferior vena cava** requires complex vascular surgery.

## Treatment

Complete **surgery**; radiotherapy in some cases; in advanced disease, **doxorubicin with trabectedin** (LMS-04 trial, which improved survival), doxorubicin with dacarbazine, gemcitabine with docetaxel, eribulin, pazopanib; surgery for selected metastases.

See also: [Sarcomas](../sarcomas/), [Uterine sarcoma](../uterine-sarcoma/).
