Oncolopedia **does not use tracking cookies or store personal data**. Searches and quiz answers are processed only in your browser: they are not sent to any server or stored.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address under its own privacy policy.

The hosting provider (Hostinger) may log technical data about visits, such as IP addresses, for security and operational reasons.

If we ever add visitor statistics or advertising, they will only be switched on with your consent, and we will explain it here.
