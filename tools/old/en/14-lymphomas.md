## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **What they are** | Cancers of lymphocytes, cells of the immune system |
| **Main groups** | Hodgkin lymphoma and non-Hodgkin lymphomas (B and T cell) |
| **Signs** | Painless swollen glands that do not go away, fever, night sweats, weight loss |
| **Diagnosis** | Biopsy of a whole lymph node |
| **Outlook** | Varies widely by type; Hodgkin lymphoma is cured in over 85% of cases |

## What they are

Lymphocytes travel in the blood and the lymphatic system, which includes the lymph nodes, spleen and bone marrow. In lymphoma, a lymphocyte becomes abnormal and multiplies out of control. The WHO classification recognises more than 80 types, grouped as follows:

- **Hodgkin lymphoma**: with characteristic Reed-Sternberg cells. Common in young adults, with an excellent outlook.
- **B-cell non-Hodgkin lymphomas**: the most common. **Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma** is aggressive but often curable; **follicular lymphoma** is slow-growing and can be controlled for years; others include mantle cell, marginal zone and Burkitt lymphoma.
- **T-cell lymphomas**: less common and more varied.

## Symptoms

- **Enlarged lymph nodes** in the neck, armpits or groin, painless and lasting more than a few weeks. Most swollen glands are due to infections.
- **B symptoms**: unexplained fever, drenching night sweats and loss of more than 10% of body weight.
- Itching, tiredness, cough or a feeling of abdominal fullness, depending on where the disease is.

## Diagnosis

The key test is a **lymph node biopsy**, ideally of the whole node, examined by pathologists experienced in lymphoma. Staging uses **PET-CT**, which is also used to assess response.

## Treatment

- **Hodgkin**: chemotherapy (such as ABVD or BEACOPP) with or without radiotherapy. On relapse, autologous transplant, antibody-drug conjugates (brentuximab) and immunotherapy.
- **Diffuse large B-cell**: chemo-immunotherapy with **rituximab** (R-CHOP or variants). On relapse, **CAR-T** and bispecific antibodies.
- **Follicular** and other slow-growing types: if there are no symptoms, sometimes only monitoring ("watch and wait"); when needed, rituximab with or without chemotherapy, and newer drugs.
