Cancer types

Nasal and sinus cancer

Rare tumours of the nose and sinuses, linked to wood dust, leather and other workplace exposures. Their symptoms are mistaken for sinusitis.

General information only. It does not replace advice from your care team and should not be used to choose a treatment.

At a glance
How common Under 1% of cancers
Risk factors Wood dust (carpenters, cabinet makers), leather, nickel, chromium, formaldehyde, tobacco
Types Squamous, intestinal-type adenocarcinoma, olfactory neuroblastoma, mucosal melanoma, sinonasal undifferentiated carcinoma (SNUC)
Symptoms One-sided blocked nose, bleeding, discharge, facial pain, double vision
Treatment Endoscopic or open surgery and radiotherapy (including proton therapy)

What it is

The paranasal sinuses (maxillary, ethmoid, frontal and sphenoid) are air-filled spaces around the nose. Intestinal-type ethmoid adenocarcinoma is strongly linked to hardwood dust and is a recognised occupational disease.

Symptoms

One-sided nasal blockage that does not improve with treatment, nosebleeds, smelly discharge, loss of smell, facial pain or swelling, watery eye, double vision or a displaced eye, loose upper teeth. As it resembles sinusitis, diagnosis is often delayed.

Treatment

  • Endoscopic nasal surgery (increasingly used) or open surgery, with skull base surgeons.
  • Radiotherapy after surgery; proton therapy spares the eyes and brain.
  • Induction chemotherapy for advanced or undifferentiated tumours.

See also: Head and neck cancer, Nasopharyngeal cancer.
