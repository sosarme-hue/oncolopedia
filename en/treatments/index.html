The tools against cancer

Surgery, radiotherapy, chemotherapy, hormone therapy, targeted therapy, immunotherapy, transplantation and palliative care: what each one is for and what to expect.

Cancer surgery

Surgery is still the treatment that cures the most solid cancers. What it is for, what margins and sentinel nodes are, and how it has moved towards less aggressive techniques.

Blood stem cell transplants and CAR-T therapies are intensive treatments for some leukaemias, lymphomas and myelomas. What they are and how they work.

Palliative care

Palliative care relieves pain and other symptoms and supports the person and their family. It is not only for the end of life: it can start at diagnosis.
