## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **How common** | 2% of leukaemias; more in men aged 50-60 |
| **Alteration** | ***BRAF* V600E** in almost 100% |
| **Symptoms** | Infections, tiredness, very large spleen, low counts of all blood cells |
| **Treatment** | Cladribine or pentostatin with rituximab; BRAF inhibitors at relapse |

## What it is

B lymphocytes with fine projections on their surface that, under the microscope, look like **hairs**. They infiltrate the marrow and spleen, causing **pancytopenia** (low red cells, white cells and platelets) and, typically, few monocytes.

## Treatment

- **Cladribine** (a single course) or pentostatin, often with **rituximab**: over 90% respond, with remissions lasting many years.
- At relapse: **vemurafenib** (a BRAF inhibitor) with rituximab, moxetumomab, ibrutinib.

## Outlook

Life expectancy is near normal with treatment.
