Cancer types

Waldenström macroglobulinaemia

An indolent lymphoma that makes an IgM protein which thickens the blood. It almost always carries the MYD88 mutation and responds to BTK inhibitors.

General information only. It does not replace advice from your care team and should not be used to choose a treatment.

At a glance
Technical name Lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma with IgM paraprotein
How common 3-4 cases per million a year; median age 70
Mutation MYD88 L265P (over 90%), CXCR4 (30-40%)
Symptoms Tiredness from anaemia, hyperviscosity (headache, blurred vision, nosebleeds), neuropathy
Treatment Monitoring; rituximab with bendamustine; BTK inhibitors (zanubrutinib, ibrutinib)

Key points

  • Excess IgM can thicken the blood (hyperviscosity syndrome), an emergency treated with plasma exchange.
  • It may cause neuropathy from antibodies against myelin (anti-MAG), cryoglobulins or amyloidosis.
  • Many symptom-free patients need only monitoring.

Treatment

Rituximab with bendamustine or with dexamethasone and cyclophosphamide; BTK inhibitors (zanubrutinib, more selective and better tolerated, ASPEN trial); proteasome inhibitors in some cases. Median survival exceeds 10 years.

See also: Multiple myeloma, Lymphomas.
