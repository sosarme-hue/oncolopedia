## Alejandro Rosàs Navarro

I am a **family physician** and a specialist in **Preventive Medicine and Public Health**, with **over 38 years in primary care**, registered with the Barcelona Medical Association under number **28574**.

In my practice I have accompanied many people through every stage of cancer: the suspicion, waiting for a biopsy, treatment, getting back to normal life and, sometimes, the end. I have also watched oncology change over these decades, with treatments that once seemed impossible. Patients' and families' questions deserve clear answers, with sources, without false hope and without alarmism.

Oncolopedia aims to make oncology accessible to everyone: what we know, how we know it and what we still don't know.

## Collaborations

For specialised clinical content, Oncolopedia will add review by professionals in medical oncology, radiation oncology, haematology and oncology nursing. If you would like to help, write to us.

## What it is and what it is not

Oncolopedia is a general-audience encyclopedia. **It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, interpretation of individual results or second opinions**, and it does not replace care from health professionals.
