## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **How common** | 3-4 cases per 100,000 a year; median age at diagnosis 68 |
| **Risk factors** | Age, previous chemotherapy or radiotherapy, tobacco, benzene, previous myelodysplastic syndromes |
| **Key genetics** | *NPM1*, *FLT3*, *IDH1/2*, *CEBPA*, *TP53*, *KMT2A* rearrangements |
| **Treatment** | Intensive chemotherapy ("7+3") ± targeted drugs; azacitidine with venetoclax for unfit patients; allogeneic transplant |
| **Survival** | 40-50% under 60; lower in older people |

## What it is

A build-up of immature **myeloblasts** that block normal marrow production. The ELN 2022 classification divides risk into **favourable** (for example, *NPM1* without *FLT3*-ITD, *CBF*), **intermediate** and **adverse** (*TP53*, complex karyotype).

## Treatment

**Patients fit for intensive treatment**:
- **Induction** with cytarabine and an anthracycline ("7+3"), adding **midostaurin** or **quizartinib** for *FLT3*, or **gemtuzumab ozogamicin** for favourable risk.
- **Consolidation** with high-dose cytarabine or **allogeneic transplant** for intermediate and adverse risk.
- **CPX-351** (liposomal daunorubicin and cytarabine) for secondary AML.

**Unfit patients** (older or with other illnesses):
- **Azacitidine with venetoclax** (VIALE-A trial), which doubled survival compared with azacitidine alone.
- **Ivosidenib** with azacitidine for *IDH1*.

**Relapse**: **gilteritinib** (*FLT3*), **enasidenib** (*IDH2*), olutasidenib (*IDH1*), and the new **menin inhibitors** (revumenib, ziftomenib) for *KMT2A* and *NPM1*.
