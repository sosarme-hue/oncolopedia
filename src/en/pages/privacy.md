Oncolopedia **uses no cookies, analytics or advertising, and collects no personal data through forms or accounts**. Searches and quiz answers are processed only in your browser: they are not sent to any server or stored.

Fonts and all other files are served from our own domain, without calls to third-party services.

There is one technical exception outside our control: as with any website, the **hosting provider (Hostinger)** automatically logs some data about each visit, such as the IP address, the date and the page requested, for security and operational reasons. We do not use those logs to identify you or build profiles.

If you email us, we will use your address and message only to reply.

If we ever add visitor statistics or advertising, they will only be switched on with your consent, and we will explain it here.
