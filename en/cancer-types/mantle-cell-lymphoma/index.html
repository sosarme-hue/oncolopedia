Cancer types

Mantle cell lymphoma

An uncommon B-cell lymphoma, mostly in older men, with the cyclin D1 translocation. BTK inhibitors have lengthened disease control, although in most cases it is still not cured.

General information only. It does not replace advice from your care team and should not be used to choose a treatment.

At a glance
How common 5-7% of non-Hodgkin lymphomas
Who Men over 60 (3-4 to 1)
Alteration t(11;14), cyclin D1; mutated TP53 indicates a worse outlook
Sites Nodes, marrow, spleen, gut (lymphomatous polyposis)
Treatment Immunochemotherapy with a BTK inhibitor; CAR-T (brexucabtagene) at relapse

Forms

  • Classic: the most common; aggressive.
  • Leukaemic non-nodal: indolent, sometimes just monitored for years.

Treatment

  • Fit younger patients: intensive immunochemotherapy with ibrutinib (TRIANGLE trial, which questions the need for autologous transplant) and rituximab maintenance.
  • Older patients: bendamustine and rituximab with acalabrutinib or ibrutinib.
  • Relapse: pirtobrutinib, venetoclax, lenalidomide, CAR-T (brexucabtagene autoleucel), glofitamab.

See also: Lymphomas, Chronic lymphocytic leukaemia.
