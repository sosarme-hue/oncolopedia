*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

Oncolopedia **does not set its own or third-party cookies** while you browse, and uses no analytics or advertising. That is why we do not show you a cookie banner.

## How to delete or block cookies

You can view, delete or block cookies in your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge…), usually under privacy.

## Changes

If we ever add non-essential cookies, such as analytics or advertising, we will ask for your permission first and update this page.
