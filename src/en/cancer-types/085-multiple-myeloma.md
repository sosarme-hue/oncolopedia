## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **How common** | 190,000 cases a year worldwide; about 6,000 in the UK |
| **Who** | Median age 70; twice as common in people of African origin |
| **Precursors** | Monoclonal gammopathy of undetermined significance (MGUS) and smouldering myeloma |
| **Typical damage (CRAB)** | High **C**alcium, **R**enal failure, **A**naemia, **B**one lesions |
| **Treatment** | Combinations of anti-CD38, proteasome inhibitors, immunomodulators and dexamethasone; autologous transplant; CAR-T and bispecifics |
| **Survival** | Median over 7-10 years for many patients |

## What it is

**Plasma cells** produce antibodies. In myeloma, a malignant clone builds up in the marrow and makes large amounts of a single protein (**paraprotein** or M protein) and **light chains**.

It is almost always preceded by **MGUS**, present in 3% of over-50s, progressing to myeloma at about 1% a year. The Icelandic iStopMM study is examining whether MGUS screening is useful.

## Symptoms

- **Bone pain** (back, ribs), fractures with minimal trauma, vertebral collapse.
- **Tiredness** from anaemia.
- **Kidney failure**.
- Thirst, confusion and constipation from high calcium.
- Repeated infections.

## Diagnosis

Serum and urine electrophoresis and **immunofixation**, serum **free light chains**, **bone marrow biopsy** with genetic testing (17p deletion, t(4;14), t(14;16), 1q gain: high risk), and **whole-body MRI or PET-CT** for bone lesions.

## Treatment

**Transplant-eligible**:
- Induction with a **quadruplet**: anti-CD38 (daratumumab or isatuximab) + bortezomib + lenalidomide + dexamethasone (PERSEUS trial).
- **High-dose chemotherapy (melphalan) with autologous transplant**.
- Lenalidomide **maintenance** (with or without anti-CD38).

**Not eligible**: **daratumumab with lenalidomide and dexamethasone** (MAIA trial) or adapted quadruplets.

**Relapse**: carfilzomib, pomalidomide, **CAR-T** (idecabtagene vicleucel, **ciltacabtagene autoleucel**), **bispecific** antibodies (teclistamab, elranatamab, talquetamab, linvoseltamab), belantamab mafodotin.

**Support**: **bisphosphonates** or denosumab for bone, pain-relieving radiotherapy, kyphoplasty, vaccination and infection prevention, kidney care.

## Outlook

In the 1990s median survival was about 3 years. Today, with modern combinations, many patients live more than 10 years, and some reach sustained **MRD-negative** status, raising the prospect of cure for some.
